# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 6, 2026

This policy applies to the Chrome extension **"X Article to Markdown"** (the "Extension").

## Data collection

**The Extension does not collect any data.**

To be explicit: the Extension does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, website content, browsing history, user activity, or any other information.

## How the Extension works

All processing happens locally in your browser:

1. When you click the Extension icon on an X article page, it reads the article content currently visible on your screen.
2. It converts that content to a Markdown file, which is saved only to the location you choose (standard browser download).
3. No data is sent to us, to X Corp., or to any third party.

## Permissions

The Extension requests two permissions:

- **`activeTab`** — grants temporary access to the currently-visible tab, but only when you actively click the Extension icon.
- **`scripting`** — allows the Extension to read the article content from that tab so it can convert it to Markdown.

## Third-party services

None. The Extension makes no network requests and integrates no third-party services, analytics, or remote code.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL and any material change will be reflected in the Extension's Chrome Web Store listing before it takes effect.
